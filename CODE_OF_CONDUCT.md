# Code of conduct

specsteward follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be welcoming and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather than people, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted in issues, pull requests, discussions or anywhere else in this project's spaces.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub ([@Dinusha-Ekanayake](https://github.com/Dinusha-Ekanayake)). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who break this code.
