# Getting help

1. **Run the doctor.** In your project: `bash .sdd/bin/sdd doctor`. It checks everything
   specsteward depends on and prints the fix for anything missing.
2. **Read the docs.** The [setup guide](docs/setup-guide.md) walks through installation, and
   the [FAQ and troubleshooting](docs/faq.md) covers the common problems with their fixes.
3. **Ask a question** in [Discussions](https://github.com/Dinusha-Ekanayake/specsteward/discussions).
4. **Report a bug** as an [issue](https://github.com/Dinusha-Ekanayake/specsteward/issues/new/choose).
   Include `bash .sdd/bin/sdd --version`, your operating system and the `sdd doctor` output.
5. **Security problems** go privately through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), never as a public
   issue.

specsteward is maintained by one person in their own time. Issues are usually answered
within a week.
